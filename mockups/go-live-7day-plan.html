7 days to a self-serve front door

One production customer (BBB) stays exactly where it is. This week ships a thin, fast onboarding surface over the existing backend, signs 2–3 new founding tenants, and puts a price on the product. Nothing else.

The three rules of the week. 1 · Platform freeze: bugfix-only everywhere existing; every idea goes to the parking lot, not the codebase. 2 · BBB is sacred: zero schema changes that touch their flows; they never see the new surface until they ask. 3 · Scope is the shell: if a screen isn't in the approved mockup, it does not exist this week.

02Business model — decided today, not debated all week

Founding tenant offer
₹999/month
  • First month free, price agreed on day one
  • Free forever under 20 customers
  • Optional ₹2,500 one-time “we set up your whole list” concierge
  • Cancel anytime · price locked for 12 months
Why these numbers

Priced against recovered revenue: one saved late payment covers the month. The future ₹999 commitment is the validation signal — a tenant who won’t agree to it was never a customer. The concierge fee makes hand-holding a product, not a cost. Value metric = customers under management, so price scales with their business, not with features.

03Onboarding spec — the 10-minute contract with yourself

  • Target: signup → first reminder sent in ≤ 10 minutes, with ≤ 30 minutes of founder time per tenant (concierge import included).
  • Zero implementation: no industry selection, no catalog setup, no KT dependency, no Test/Live choice. Defaults everywhere; correct later in Settings.
  • Import is the whole game: paste-from-Excel → parsed rows → one confirm screen. Whatever mess arrives on WhatsApp, you (or an agent pass) turn into the paste format — that’s the concierge.
  • Activation metric: tenant has ≥ 5 customers scheduled and ≥ 1 reminder delivered. That number is the only dashboard that matters this week.
