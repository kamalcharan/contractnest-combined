Contracts

A plan tells you what's due. A contract adds what the buyer gets, for how long, and their sign-off — extended to them with a CNAK, so it lives on both sides.

🔑How the two-way works: every sent contract gets a CNAK (a short access key) + 4-digit secret. Share it on WhatsApp — the buyer opens it with no app and no login, accepts on their phone, and the same link becomes their live contract page. If the buyer uses CollectNest too, entering the CNAK claims the contract into their workspace — one contract, both sides, always in sync.
4 contracts · ₹2,13,500 total value

What a contract adds over a plan

  • Service blocks — what the buyer gets, itemised (sessions, visits, access), not just an amount.
  • A term — start, end, and a renewal date the reminder engine already knows how to chase.
  • Acceptance — the buyer's recorded yes: name, time, and CNAK on a one-page document you can both verify.
  • The same payment schedule — collections doesn't change; it now hangs off something both sides agreed to.
