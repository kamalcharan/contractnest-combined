EARLIER — Billing count is QUANTITY, not the calendar
   (owner-confirmed rule, 2026-08-16)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE RULE: quantity is the COUNT, the cycle is the SPACING.
  qty 1 + 15-day cycle  = 1 occurrence
  qty 3 + 15-day cycle  = 3 occurrences, 15 days apart
Price already followed this (totalPrice = unitPrice × quantity, set
in ServiceBlocksStep) — the billing engine did not.

THE BUG (found on test CN-1001): when qty <= 1 the engine took the
COUNT from the calendar (ceil(term ÷ period) = 13) while the PRICE
still came from quantity (unit × 1 = ₹600) — two different sources
— so ₹600 was DIVIDED into 13 × ₹46.15 instead of billed once. Same
class as CN-1002: pricing and scheduling disagreeing.

  - contractnest-ui/src/utils/service-contracts/contractEvents.ts     [FULL file]
  - contractnest-api/src/services/contractEventsDerivationService.ts  [FULL file]
    BOTH copies changed in lockstep, verified byte-identical.
    Recurring-billing branch now: count = quantity.
    ONE exception kept, and only one: a visit-generating block whose
    BILLING cycle DIFFERS from its SERVICE cycle holds two genuinely
    different schedules that one quantity field cannot express —
    there quantity = VISITS and the bill count derives from the term
    ("18 visits every 18 days, billed monthly, 1 year" = 18 visits,
    12 bills — the previously-approved behaviour, UNCHANGED). When
    the two cycles MATCH they are one schedule: bills = visits = qty.
    The dead ceil() fallback (countRecurringPeriods) is no longer
    called from the billing branch.

    Cadence gate + SQL backstop needed NO change and were not
    touched: for matching cycles the service-leg check already tests
