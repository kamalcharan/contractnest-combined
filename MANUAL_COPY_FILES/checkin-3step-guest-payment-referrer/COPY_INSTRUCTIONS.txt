WHAT THIS IS
---------------------------------------------------------------
Implements the 3-step check-in redesign we mocked up and agreed on
(Identify -> Payment -> Check-in), plus everything that came out of
the audit thread:

1) Identify is now its own step -- no more auto-populated identity
   bleeding into payment/attendance. Uses the exact same phone-entry /
   not-found guest-or-substitute / device-recognition flow as before,
   just laid out as step 1 instead of interleaved with attendance.

2) Payment is its own step, and now branches correctly by identity:
   - Member (or a substitute standing in for one): the real "Your
     dues" card, same as before.
   - Guest: NO LONGER shows the member dues card (that was a bug you
     caught -- a guest with no contract was seeing a fake dues
     screen). Guests now pick from standalone catalog services the
     tenant flags guestPayable -- nothing hardcoded. Your existing
     "Guest Participation Fee" (Rs.600) shows up here automatically;
     add more anytime by setting config.guestPayable: true on any
     other service-category catalog block.
   - Substitute: no payment step at all (matches gs_checkin_substitute
     today -- it only ever recorded attendance, never a payment; that
     wasn't changed here).
   - A ContractNest platform brand card sits above the tenant's own
     branding on every screen (blue, "Secure session check-in", a
     Verified badge) -- the "brand propagation" card you asked for.

3) Guest "Referred by" -- a required search-and-select field (typing
   filters real roster members, tenant + membership scoped) in the
   guest quick-details form. Tags which member brought the guest.

4) "Payments to confirm" fix -- solved going forward. Root cause was
   confirmed from your own audit: payEventId/selectedServiceId
   auto-populate just so the "current due" / "pick a service" card can
   render -- they were never a signal that the member/guest actually
   wants to pay. submit() now only creates a payment declaration when
   there's a real signal: tapping "Pay now" (opens UPI) or typing a
   UPI reference. Simply checking in with an open due sitting there no
   longer creates a phantom pending declaration. This supersedes the
   earlier checkin-payment-declaration-fix batch (still sitting
   uncopied in that older folder) -- that fix is now folded into this
   file; you can ignore/delete that older batch, nothing further
   needed from it.

IMPORTANT -- guest fees are TRACKED-ONLY, not in the ledger yet
---------------------------------------------------------------
This was a deliberate scope call (asked you, you picked "track-only
for now"): confirming a guest-fee declaration marks it confirmed, same
as any other declaration, but does NOT create an invoice or revenue
entry -- there's no real contract for it to settle against. It's
recorded as check-in data, not accounting data. If/when you want real
ledger integration for guest fees (a lightweight walk-in-sale/invoice
path), that's separate follow-up work, flagged for later.
