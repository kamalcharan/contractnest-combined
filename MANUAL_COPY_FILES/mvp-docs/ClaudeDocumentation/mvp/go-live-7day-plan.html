ContractNest · Express — AMC · Manufacturing · Pest Control · Housekeeping

7 days to a self-serve front door

The pipeline is recurring field-service businesses: AMC vendors, manufacturers, pest control, housekeeping. Different industries, one identical shape — recurring visits + recurring billing + renewals — so one express product serves all four with vocabulary as the only difference. This week ships a UX-only surface over the existing API, edge functions and tables: no new endpoints, no migrations. Its first users are BBB’s own chapters (each chapter is its own tenant — 39 to onboard), which de-risks the surface on a paying customer before a cold prospect sees it, then 2–3 pipeline prospects convert as founding tenants.

The three rules of the week. 1 · Platform freeze: bugfix-only everywhere existing; every idea goes to the parking lot, not the codebase. 2 · UX-only: no new API route, edge function, table or column. Lite is a new client of the existing backend — which is what makes BBB structurally safe. 3 · Scope is this page: if a screen isn’t listed here, it does not exist this week.

01The wedge, adjusted for B2B field-service contracts

Collections is still the money moment, but in this segment three things gate it that don’t exist in B2C: renewals (contracts lapse silently and the book quietly shrinks), proof of service (the visit log is what wins the payment argument — and in pest control it doubles as the compliance record a restaurant shows its FSSAI auditor), and invoice-shaped payments (NEFT/cheque with UTR, credit periods, an accountant on the other side). So the express MVP is: contract → visit + payment schedules → visit check-off with photo → payment & renewal chasing → CNAK page the client can see. Fewer, larger contracts than fitness — which makes the client-side page more valuable, not less: a society secretary, plant manager, or restaurant owner who can see visits and dues stops calling you for statements.

03Business model — decided today, not debated all week

Founding tenant offer
₹1,999/month
  • First month free, price agreed in writing on day one
  • Free under 10 contracts
  • Optional ₹5,000 one-time “we set up your entire AMC book” concierge
  • Cancel anytime · price locked 12 months
Why these numbers

An AMC vendor with 80 contracts averaging ₹40k holds a ₹32L book. One un-lapsed renewal or one dispute won by a visit log pays for years of the product. Priced against renewals saved + collections recovered, with contracts-under-management as the value metric. The written ₹1,999 commitment is the validation signal; the concierge fee turns hand-holding into product.

04Onboarding spec — the 10-minute contract with yourself

  • Target: signup → first reminder or renewal alert in ≤ 10 minutes, with ≤ 30 minutes of founder time per tenant (concierge import included).
  • Zero implementation: no industry taxonomy setup, no KT curation, no catalog studio, no Test/Live choice. KT only pre-fills known equipment blocks; a vendor with unlisted equipment types their blocks by hand and loses nothing.
  • Import is the whole game: their AMC register lives in Excel/Tally exports. Paste → parsed rows → one confirm screen. Whatever arrives on WhatsApp, you (or an agent pass) convert to the paste format — that’s the concierge.
  • Activation metric: tenant has ≥ 5 contracts loaded with schedules and ≥ 1 reminder or renewal alert delivered. The only dashboard that matters this week.
