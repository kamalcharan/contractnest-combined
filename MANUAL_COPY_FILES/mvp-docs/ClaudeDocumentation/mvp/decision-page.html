The core call

✓
Build the product-led shell you already designed. Do not redesign anything. It already has persona → industry → seeded catalog, JIT questions, coach marks, NPS, landing, portal, and a 4-step wizard. The design work is done.
✓
In 7 days, build only what does not exist yet. Onboarding, an express contract lane, and hide-until-earned rules. Redesigning live screens is the roadmap, not the sprint — that's what protects BBB.

Five open questions — my answer to each

1 · Does the sprint touch existing screens?
No. Add new screens only; leave Dashboard, Contracts, Contact view, AR/AP exactly as they are. Zero risk to BBB, and you can ship without a regression pass you don't have time for.
2 · Collapse the 13-step wizard now?
No — put a 5-field express lane beside it. The wizard is what BBB uses. A new short path costs days; rewriting the wizard costs weeks and risks a live tenant.
3 · Who onboards first?
BBB chapters #2 and #3, on Day 3. Each chapter is its own tenant, so the 39-chapter rollout and this build are the same work. A paying customer finds your bugs before a cold prospect does.
4 · Price?
₹1,999/month, agreed in writing on day one, first month free. Free under 10 contracts. ₹5,000 one-time to load their book. The written commitment is the signal — not the revenue.
5 · VaNi now?
No — week 3+. It's the moat, not the wedge. It already exists in three disconnected places (scanner, wizard draft, block recommender on an idle branch). Converging them is its own project.
