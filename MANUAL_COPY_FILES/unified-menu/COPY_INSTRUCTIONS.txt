BATCH: unified-menu
ONE menu (industryMenus.ts) in the lite look. LITE_MENUS deleted.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY
---
There were TWO menu systems — industryMenus.ts (full, nested submenus)
and LITE_MENUS in liteAccess.ts (flat, ✦ sections) — rendered by a
`liteTier ? … : …` branch in Sidebar. Every menu change had to be made
twice, and they drifted. Four separate mechanisms decided whether an
item appeared: adminOnly, reveal rules, the lite branch, perspective.

WHAT CHANGED  (UI only — no API, no DB)
---------------------------------------
1. ONE MENU — industryMenus.ts, flat, in the lite look.
   Sections: YOUR WORKSPACE · GROW WITH CONTRACTNEST · CONFIGURE
     workspace: Dashboard, Contracts, Claim Contract (highlighted),
                Requests, Service Events, Appointments, Group Sessions,
                Contacts, Equipment Registry, Facility Registry,
                Finance (AR/AP)
     grow:      Catalog Studio, VaNi (purple highlight)
     configure: Settings
   Submenus are gone from the regular menu (admin keeps its nesting).

2. MenuItem gains: section, requires (FeatureKey), perspectives,
   tierOverrides, copyKey, highlight ('vani' | 'brand').

3. ONE ACCESS RULE — getMenuAccess(item, {tier, perspective, revealed})
   → 'open' | 'locked' | 'hidden'. Replaces all four old mechanisms.
   A lite tenant sees the SAME menu; unavailable items render with ✦ and
   open the existing TrialCrossSellModal instead of navigating. Hiding
   was replaced by locking wherever it makes sense — the upsell IS the
   menu.
   Lite-open capabilities: cnak → events, registry.  rfq → rfq.
   Full tenant → everything open.

4. SIDEBAR — one render path. The lite branch, LiteNavItem and
   VaNiNavItem are deleted; FlatNavItem renders every item (open /
   locked / highlighted). Trial bar shows only when a tier is set.
   Admin block unchanged.

5. liteAccess.ts — LITE_MENUS and LiteMenuEntry DELETED. Cross-sell
   copy, restricted routes, capabilities, trial copy and LITE_WALKOVER
   all stay.

6. OWNER CALLS (2026-08-03): "VaNi (old)" (12 reference pages) removed
   from the menu — routes and files untouched. "Claim Contract" is now a
   top-level highlighted item (brand orange, like VaNi's purple).
