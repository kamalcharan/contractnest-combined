WHAT IT IS
  · classification 'lead' on t_contacts (chip "Lead" in Contacts, teal)
  · t_contact_interests = the history: package, source (website widget / link /
    VaNi / by hand), what they asked, stage new → contacted → converted | lost
  · /leads (Revenue side, menu after Contacts):
      From your reach  — one row per contact with its interests; stage chips;
                         Follow up (note → contacted) · Send contract (wizard
                         with the buyer chosen) · Lost · Open contact; Add a lead
      Asked you        — RFQs other tenants invited THIS tenant to, matched by
                         the invite's mobile/email against the tenant's business
                         phone/WhatsApp/email and its users; Respond with quote
  · conversion is automatic: any contract created for the contact (storefront
    purchase, the wizard) converts the open interests and swaps lead → client
  · the checkout names the buyer right after the OTP (POST /identify), so a
    checkout that stops there still leaves a lead ("started, didn't buy")
