─── WHAT CHANGED ────────────────────────────────────
 BOARD (/ops/cockpit)
 · jtd_ops_board (017) unions INVOICE ROWS using Money In's own rule for a
   whole-invoice due (open receivable invoice, balance > 0, contract with no
   live billing event; skipped when an open payment job already points at the
   invoice). Kinds invoice_overdue (needs you) / invoice_ahead (coming due),
   anchored on the due date, amount = balance. No ladder (no payment job):
   the tools are Send invoice · email / WhatsApp — the EXISTING per-invoice
   send behind the /invoices viewer (POST /api/invoices/:id/send →
   fn_enqueue_invoice_notification) — and View invoice. "sent 2× · last email
   3 Sep" on the evidence line counts those sends. The feed shows them.
 · Every collections card that carries an invoice now shows its number next
   to History / Open, and the number opens the invoice (contract route when
   there is a contract, /invoices/:id otherwise — one viewer serves both).
 · Vocabulary: Service overdue / today / in progress / scheduled, "service 2
   of 18", Start service, "3 services fall due", "2 services have no
   technician yet". Empty states reworded. New group chip "Invoices overdue";
   "Coming due" includes invoice_ahead; headline gains "N invoices overdue".
 · A refused send (rule_disabled) toasts with a link to Automation Rules,
   as the invoice viewer does.
 REGISTER (/ops/services)
 · Lanes: All · Collections · Services · Appointments · Follow-ups — the
   board's words plus the two records only this page keeps.
   Collections rows carry the invoice number (from the receivables payload
   Money In and /invoices already read — no new backend) in the last column,
   opening the invoice; OPEN whole-invoice dues appear as rows ("Invoice
   INV-10052 · whole invoice · ₹2,29,500 open") with the invoice status pill;
   an instalment with no invoice yet says so.
   Appointments = get_appointments_list (the old kanban's data as a record):
   Contract · Service · Slot · Status (Asked / Confirmed / Customer proposed /
   No response / Not needed / Completed / Cancelled) · Who · "On the board".
   Follow-ups = jtd_tasks (017): every call task open or closed — Follow-up
   (set for yourself) vs Call assigned, due date, Overdue / Due today / Open /
   Done · outcome, Who, invoice or "set by", Kind select.
   When / Who / search / Show closed / By customer apply to every lane.
 · "Custom Cycle 1/13 1/13" fixed (label already carried the count).
 · Activity: every kind chip stays visible at zero (a missing chip read as
   "no appointments"); default window is the last 90 days; group "Visits"
   relabelled "Services".
 · Tab renamed Events → Commitments.
