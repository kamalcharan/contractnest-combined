─── WHAT CHANGED ────────────────────────────────────
 ONE ROW MODEL. The reader now returns every open payment job as exactly one
 card with
   kind    its state — declaration_pending · send_failed · call_open · paused ·
           rung_due · payment_ahead · rung_ahead · ladder_exhausted ·
           overdue_no_ladder (+ awaiting_activation for contracts)
   anchor  WHEN it wants attention (declared at · failed at · call assigned ·
           promised date · rung due time · due date)
   bucket  anchor − today: overdue · today · next few days · next weeks ·
           later · parked (paused with no date)
 ONE CARD. components/ops/JobCard.tsx renders every row in BOTH views; the
 `compact` flag changes density only. actionsFor(kind) is the single place
 buttons come from — a coming-due payment gets Email · WhatsApp · Log a call ·
 Assign call · Pause · Open like a reminder-due one. Nudging before the due
 date is recorded by the tool as an off-ladder heads-up (rung 0); the ladder
 still fires on schedule.
 FILTERS + PAGING run in the reader so counts always agree: kind chips ·
 next-rung channel · overdue age (0–7 / 8–30 / 31–90 / 90+) · billing cycle ·
 Team / Mine / Unassigned (call tasks) · search · horizon 7 / 14 / 30 / 90 or
 a custom from–to. Every chip/option shows how many rows picking it yields
 (facet counts ignore their own dimension). Each bucket returns its total and
 the first 20; "Show 20 more" grows that bucket only.
 CONTROLS moved to the top (visible on load): VaNi chip · horizon · List/Lanes
 · search · filter row. The "collections · ladder 0e · 3e … automation off"
 line is gone: the ladder is the tooltip on the rung pill, and the VaNi chip
 says the truth — rule on → "VaNi · payment reminders on"; VaNi on + rule
 off → "VaNi could send these N reminders · turn on Payment reminders";
 VaNi off → "Let VaNi send these N reminders" → /vani/landing.
 HEADLINE now names both: "29 need you — 29 reminders due. 49 payments and
 N reminders fall due in the next 30 days."

─── STEP 5 ADDED 2026-09-17 — Follow up ─────────────
 "Follow up" = a dated task assigned to YOURSELF through the existing
 Assign-call tool (owner: not a bookmark; distinct from an appointment).
 · migration jtd-nucleus/011 (LIVE): jtd_escalate_payment_call gains
   p_due_at (the task's scheduled_at; business_context.task_kind =
   'follow_up' when you assign yourself, else 'escalation'; a date more than
   a day in the past is refused: due_in_past). The 7-arg overload was DROPPED
   so the call is unambiguous. The board anchors an open call on its due
   date, so "follow up on Tuesday" sits in the right column and turns
   overdue when Tuesday passes; call_task carries due_at + kind; the feed
   carries task_kind + due_at.
 · API: POST …/escalate accepts due_at (YYYY-MM-DD → 10:00 IST, or ISO).
 · UI: every card that can assign a call gets "Follow up" (date, default
   tomorrow → "Set follow-up"); Assign call gets an optional "Due by" date;
   the pill reads "Your follow-up" / "Follow-up" / "Call assigned"; the
   evidence line reads "follow-up by you · due 20 Sep"; the feed reads
   "<you> set a follow-up on <buyer> for 20 Sep". Log a call closes it.
 Probed live (rolled back): follow-up 3 days out → kind call_open, bucket
 "next 3 days", Mine 1; second follow-up on the same job → refused
 (call_already_open); past date → refused (due_in_past).
 Extra checklist:

─── STEP 6 ADDED 2026-09-17 — one activity timeline per contract ─────
 The contract page's Audit tab read only the service-execution audit log,
 so none of the collections activity showed; the cockpit had no history.
 · migration jtd-nucleus/012 (LIVE, applied as 012 + 012b):
   jtd_contract_activity(tenant, contract, is_live, sources[], limit, offset)
   unions, for ONE contract: service (t_audit_log), billing
   (t_contract_event_audit of its billing events), collections (every n_jtd
   communication/task about it — by contract_id, by its payment-job ids, or
   by business_context.contract_id, which is all the scanner's automated
   payment_due rows carry; ladder paused/resumed history; declarations as
   two rows — declared, then confirmed/rejected). One row shape (when, who,
   title, from→to, channel, status, amount, ids), newest first, paged,
   per-source counts. Never totals.
 · API: GET /api/jtd/collections/contracts/:contractId/activity
        ?sources=service,billing,collections&limit=50&offset=0
 · UI:
   contractnest-ui/src/components/ops/HistoryDrawer.tsx (NEW) — right-hand
     drawer: All / Collections / Billing events / Service chips with counts,
     timeline rows with icon by kind, status colour, "Show 30 more",
     "Open contract". Opened from the new "History" link next to Open on
     EVERY card, both views (JobCard onHistory; Commitments renders it).
   contractnest-ui/src/components/contracts/AuditTab.tsx (EDITED) — keeps
     its five categories on the old reader byte-for-byte; adds "Collections"
     and "Billing events" from the activity reader; "All" is the union; the
     summary strip and chip counts include them.
 Probed live: CN-1001 → 7 rows (2 declarations by CHARAN KAMAL, 1
 confirmation, 4 billing-event changes); the nudged contract → "Reminder on
 WhatsApp to NISHIKANT MHAISEKHAR · rung 1 [sent · Sanjay Ghotikar]";
