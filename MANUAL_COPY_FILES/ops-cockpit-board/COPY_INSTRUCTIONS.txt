─── WHAT CHANGED ────────────────────────────────────
 ONE ROW MODEL. The reader now returns every open payment job as exactly one
 card with
   kind    its state — declaration_pending · send_failed · call_open · paused ·
           rung_due · payment_ahead · rung_ahead · ladder_exhausted ·
           overdue_no_ladder (+ awaiting_activation for contracts)
   anchor  WHEN it wants attention (declared at · failed at · call assigned ·
           promised date · rung due time · due date)
   bucket  anchor − today: overdue · today · next few days · next weeks ·
           later · parked (paused with no date)
 ONE CARD. components/ops/JobCard.tsx renders every row in BOTH views; the
 `compact` flag changes density only. actionsFor(kind) is the single place
 buttons come from — a coming-due payment gets Email · WhatsApp · Log a call ·
 Assign call · Pause · Open like a reminder-due one. Nudging before the due
 date is recorded by the tool as an off-ladder heads-up (rung 0); the ladder
 still fires on schedule.
 FILTERS + PAGING run in the reader so counts always agree: kind chips ·
 next-rung channel · overdue age (0–7 / 8–30 / 31–90 / 90+) · billing cycle ·
 Team / Mine / Unassigned (call tasks) · search · horizon 7 / 14 / 30 / 90 or
 a custom from–to. Every chip/option shows how many rows picking it yields
 (facet counts ignore their own dimension). Each bucket returns its total and
 the first 20; "Show 20 more" grows that bucket only.
 CONTROLS moved to the top (visible on load): VaNi chip · horizon · List/Lanes
 · search · filter row. The "collections · ladder 0e · 3e … automation off"
 line is gone: the ladder is the tooltip on the rung pill, and the VaNi chip
 says the truth — rule on → "VaNi · payment reminders on"; VaNi on + rule
 off → "VaNi could send these N reminders · turn on Payment reminders";
 VaNi off → "Let VaNi send these N reminders" → /vani/landing.
 HEADLINE now names both: "29 need you — 29 reminders due. 49 payments and
 N reminders fall due in the next 30 days."
