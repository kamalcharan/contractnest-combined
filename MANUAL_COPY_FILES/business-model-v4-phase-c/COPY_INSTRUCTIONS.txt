WHAT SOFT MEANS HERE
-------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTHING IS BLOCKED. This phase adds no constraint, no RAISE, no
rejected insert, no disabled button. create_contract_transaction is
untouched. A tenant over their allowance creates the contract, and the
meter still moves.

What changes is that the tenant is TOLD:
  - a toast when the contract wizard OPENS (not on submit - so the
    warning arrives before six steps are filled in, not after), with
    "See plans" as the action
  - a banner on the Subscription page whose copy says outright that
    nothing is blocked, because a tenant who thinks they are locked out
    stops working instead of upgrading
  - "17 of 3 used, 14 over" rather than "limit reached", which would
    read like a wall they already stopped at

A LIMIT OF 0 IS NOT "OVER". A seller plan sets limit_rfqs = 0 on
purpose. Zero with zero usage renders as "not in this plan" and raises
no flag; it only counts as over once something is actually created
against it. The first cut of the trigger read 0 >= 0 as over-limit and
would have nagged every seller-plan tenant forever.

WHEN THE PLAN EXPIRES, CREDITS STAY. auto_expire_contracts already
flips plan contracts active -> expired nightly. The new trigger zeroes
limit_contracts and limit_rfqs for that tenant and touches nothing
else - credits, grant rates and add-ons are left exactly as they were.
Tested live on CN-1001 and restored.
