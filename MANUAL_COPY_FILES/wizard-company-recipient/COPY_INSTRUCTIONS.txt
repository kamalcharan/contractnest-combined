Owner: "it has contact number and mobile name — if there is a restriction,
in the 2nd step only we have to alert saying add the contact person";
"fix it".

Submodule affected: contractnest-ui only. Three files. No route, API, edge
or DB change. Existing draft save path, toasts and loaders untouched.

─── WHAT WAS WRONG ──────────────────────────────────
 CN-1010 (signia → "buyer b", live draft) stopped at chapter 04 Delivery &
 acceptance with "Not saved. The selected contact person is missing. Return
 to Agreement to choose the recipient." "buyer b" is a corporate contact
 with a mobile and an email and, at the time, no contact person. Delivery
 required, for any corporate recipient, either a chosen person or the
 "use company contact" flag whenever acceptance is not automatic; the flag
 is only set by a button inside the collapsed "Contact person & delivery
 details" section of Agreement, and Agreement itself never asked for it.
 The draft's saved wizard state: buyerId set, buyerContactPersonId null,
 useCompanyContact false, acceptanceMethod signoff → blocked. The message
 was wrong too: no person had ever been selected and none existed.

─── WHAT CHANGED ────────────────────────────────────
 steps/BuyerSelectionStep.tsx
 · New effect: when the loaded corporate contact has zero contact persons
   (and no person / company flag is set yet), the company contact is
   selected automatically — same wiring as the existing "auto-select the
   primary person" effect, no toast. The "Using Company Contact Info" card
   then shows in the picker as before. Guarded against the change-buyer
   flow and the loading state; runs once per contact.
 experience/AgreementPage.tsx
 · The contact card's caption now says, in that chapter: "No contact person
   — the agreement goes to the company's own mobile and email. Add a contact
   person in Contacts if you want a named recipient." (was "Company contact
   selected"). State-driven; no extra fetch.
 experience/DeliveryPage.tsx
 · A person is required to EXIST only when one was chosen in Agreement
   (needsPerson = corporate && buyerContactPersonId). Otherwise the contact
   itself is the acceptance recipient and its own channels are shown —
   matching what the send handler already does (buyer_contact_person_id,
   else buyer_id). The only remaining refusal is a chosen person that has
   since been removed: "The contact person chosen in Agreement is no
   longer on this contact. Return to Agreement to choose the recipient."
 · CN-1010 as saved today passes this chapter without going back.

 Not changed (noted): a contact person with no mobile/email — e.g. the
 "buytest" person just linked under "buyer b" — will show "No email on this
 contact…" in Delivery, and the send falls back to that person's channels
 only (edge contracts handler, lookupId = buyer_contact_person_id first).
 Give the person a mobile/email in Contacts, or leave the company contact
 selected.
