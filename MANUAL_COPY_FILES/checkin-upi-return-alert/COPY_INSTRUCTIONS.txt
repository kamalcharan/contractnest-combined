BATCH: checkin-upi-return-alert
Pre-departure "come back" alert on the check-in UPI pay flow
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT
----
Owner-flagged risk (2026-08-02): a member taps "Open UPI app", GPay
swallows them, and they never return to enter the UPI reference — the
payment happens but the check-in has nothing to reconcile. The
return-nudge banner only fires IF they come back; nothing warned them
BEFORE leaving.

Now, tapping "Open UPI app" first raises a bottom-sheet alert:
  Pay, then come back here 👋
  1. Your UPI ID is copied — pay ₹X in your UPI app ("Pay to UPI ID")
  2. Note the UPI reference number from the payment screen
  3. Return to this page and enter it — your check-in is not recorded
     until you do
  [Got it — open UPI app]   [Cancel]

Only the confirm button copies the VPA, marks the attempt and fires the
bare upi:// launch (same behavior as before, just gated). Cancel/tap-
outside backs out cleanly. Existing "Back from paying?" return nudge is
untouched — the two bracket the round trip.
