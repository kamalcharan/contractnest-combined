===============================================================

BUG FOUND (from your "31 payments to confirm" audit):
Of the 31 pending payment declarations, only 3 had a real UPI
reference filled in -- the other 28 had upi_reference = null. Root
cause confirmed in code, not a one-off: in SessionCheckinPage.tsx,
`payEventId` auto-populates the instant a member has ANY open due
(so the "current due" card can render) -- it was never actually a
signal that the member wants to pay. The submit() function gated
creating a payment declaration purely on `payEventId` being non-empty,
so simply checking in for attendance (with any open due, which is
most members) silently created a phantom pending declaration every
time, whether the member ever touched the payment section, tapped
"Pay now", or typed a reference. This also explains why some members
(e.g. Bharat Kumar Mangipudi) show TWO pending declarations for the
same due -- re-opening/resubmitting created a second one, since there
was never a real gate.

FIX: a payment declaration is now only created when there's an actual
signal of payment intent -- the member tapped "Pay now" (opens their
UPI app) OR typed a UPI reference. Just having an open due sitting
there (payEventId auto-set) no longer triggers a declaration on its
own. Also fixes the same gap in "dues-only mode" (no session today).
