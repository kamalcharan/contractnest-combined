BATCH: checkin-upi-qr-flow (2026-09-16)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
Closes the "check-in UPI pay link fails on real GPay" item open since
2026-07-25. Root cause isolated live this session with real ₹1 payments —
see ROOT CAUSE. The earlier checkin-upi-merchant-qr batch's payload fix
(org_id/mcc in the link) was correct and stays; it is not the problem.

Submodules affected: contractnest-ui ONLY.
No API, edge, RPC or DB change. No new dependency.

─── ROOT CAUSE ──────────────────────────────────────

The payload was never the problem once org_id/mcc were added. What GPay
refuses for BBB's merchant-registered VPA (9849502193@kbl, U S R Travels,
mc=4722) is the DELIVERY MECHANISM:

  upi:// INTENT handed to GPay by a browser/other app
      → GPay opens, shows the amount, then FAILS at payment
        ("Payments to this receiver are not allowed by UPI network")

  Identical string scanned by the member INSIDE GPay as a QR
      → pays.

Proved live, same phone, same account, same minute:
  - physical bank sticker scanned inside GPay        → ₹1 PAID
  - same sticker via a QR-reader app → link → GPay    → fails at payment
  - our exact generated payload (amount + reference + org_id/mcc) rendered
    as a QR and scanned inside GPay                   → ₹1 PAID
  - same payload as the check-in page's tap link      → fails

Personal VPAs are different: the intent path DOES work for
kamalcharan@okicici (GPay accepted the link; a separate GPay-side ₹1,000
per-transaction cap applied — the payer app's rule, not ours). So the
intent button stays for personal-VPA tenants.

The check-in page does `window.location.href = 'upi://…'` — the blocked
path. No link we can build works for a merchant VPA. A member cannot scan
their own screen, and "save the QR → open GPay → pick from gallery" was
rejected by the owner as friction. The chapter's printed bank QR at the
desk is the natural gesture, so the page points at it.

─── WHAT CHANGES (SessionCheckinPage.tsx, renderPayBlock) ───

 When the tenant's payment config carries merchant fields (org_id AND mcc):
   - The "Open UPI app" intent button and its come-back alert are NOT
     rendered (they can only fail).
   - In their place, one card: "Scan the chapter QR at the desk", the
     amount large, and one line — open GPay/PhonePe/Paytm, scan the
     printed payment QR, check it shows <payee>, enter <amount>, pay.
   - Primary button "I've paid — enter my UPI reference" → the EXISTING
     reference entry, duplicate checks and declaration flow, unchanged.
     paymentAttempted is set only by that explicit tap (consistent with
     the 2026-08-06 fix that stopped fabricated declarations).
   - Footer line tells the member check-in is recorded once the
     reference is entered, and that this account takes QR payments only.
   - The "App link failed, or already paid?" disclosure reads
     "No QR nearby, or already paid?" on this path.
 When there are NO merchant fields (personal VPA, e.g. BBB's test config):
   - Zero behaviour change.
 Untouched everywhere: Copy-UPI-ID box, already-declared-today warning,
 paid gate, duplicate alerts, guest-service step wiring (same function).

 TRANSACTION CAPTURE IS PRESERVED: member → amount → UPI reference →
 declaration for the chair, exactly as before. What is lost, for this VPA
 only, is the one-tap amount-pre-filled launch — which never worked here.
 The link code stays correct, so if the bank enables intent payments on
 the merchant VPA the button can simply be re-enabled.
