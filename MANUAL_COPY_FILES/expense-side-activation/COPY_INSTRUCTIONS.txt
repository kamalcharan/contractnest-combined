WHAT THIS BATCH DOES

1) REVENUE → EXPENSE SHIFT (mirror of the Expense→Revenue gate)
   • Toggle to Expense now probes readiness: ≥1 OWN registry asset
     (ownership_type='self'; a seller's CLIENT assets deliberately don't
     count). Lazy, limit-1, cached, fail-open — same as the revenue probe.
   • Empty side → the same empty-state modal, direction-aware copy:
     "Expense is your buying side… tell VaNi what you own and she builds
     your registry." CTA → onboarding lite; Cancel keeps Revenue.

2) SIDE-AWARE ACTIVATION (the important semantic fix, BOTH directions)
   Activation runs seed ONLY the activated side's leg, and /start/serve
   asks that side's question:
   • Expense activation: "What do you own?" (buyer wording), seeds
     businessType='buyer' → own registry only. WITHOUT this, persona
     'both' would trigger the dual-intent rule and seed everything the
     tenant SERVICES into what they OWN — wrong data.
   • Revenue activation: seller wording, seeds businessType='seller' →
     catalog only (also stops the previous batch's mild side effect of
     adding serviced picks to a buyer's own registry).
   • Hand-off via sessionStorage ('cn_activate_side'): written when the
     activation CTA is clicked, read by BusinessPersonaStep (pre-selects
     "Both") and ServiceableStep (wording + seed leg), threaded to
     VaniWorkingStep (tasks, seed call, tail route, copy), cleared at the
     done screen. Fresh signups never set it → behave exactly as before.
   • Activation tails: expense → equipment-confirm → lov-setup → done;
     revenue → pricing-review → terms → (assets) → lov-setup → done.

3) BUYER SIGNUP POLISH (Expense onboarding at signup)
   • journey.ts: buyer rail no longer promises "First contract" and
     "Your plan" — steps a buyer never reaches (the VENDOR authors
     contracts; /start/contract cannot work without a catalog). Buyer
     rail: Business → What you own → Building → Your assets → Your
     lists → Workspace ready.
   • VaniDoneStep 9B: RFQ and CNAK now read as the buyer's two equal
     first actions — primary CTA "Ask vendors to quote (RFQ) →", plus
     "I have a contract code (CNAK) →"; signoff copy explains both.
   • Deferred on purpose: guided first-RFQ rehearsal, CNAK-lite
     workspace (#4), plan screen for buyers.
