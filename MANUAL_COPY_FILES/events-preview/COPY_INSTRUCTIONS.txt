IMPORTANT BOUNDARIES
The existing scheduling engine is unchanged. It uses fixed day-count term rules
(months = 30 days; years = 365 days). The UI labels the result a schedule window
and explains it in 'How are these dates calculated?'; it does not substitute the
HTML demo's calendar-month assumptions. Money reconciliation is retained.
Group Sessions stay on their shared occurrence schedule, not duplicated here.
Ongoing services have no fixed service occurrences; applicable payments remain.
Large timelines show 25 date groups initially, then Show more dates.
Extremely large/damaged drafts above the interactive preview bound (50,000
estimated quantity-plus-term days) report an error rather than freeze the page.
Saving Events Preview remains a draft operation. Final review is the NEXT release.
The review receipt is a UI acknowledgement, NOT a legal or security signature.
Future final review must revalidate the plan/receipt and live context before send.

No merge/commit/push commands yet. Test locally and confirm first. Unrelated
working changes and other submodules must remain untouched.
