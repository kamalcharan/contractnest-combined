WHAT THIS DOES
──────────────
When a tenant in Expense mode taps Revenue on the header toggle:
  1. The modal opens in a brief "Checking your Revenue side…" state while
     a lazy readiness probe runs (≥1 catalog block via the existing
     catalog-studio blocks endpoint, limit 1, cached per tenant+env).
  2. Revenue side HAS data  → the original confirm dialog, unchanged.
  3. Revenue side is EMPTY  → a proper empty state: inline illustration
     (the toggle with the Revenue pill dashed/waiting), a plain-language
     explanation of both sides (Revenue = contracts you send: catalog,
     prices, receivables · Expense = contracts you receive: assets, dues,
     payables), and two actions:
       "Set up my Revenue side · ~6 min"  → routes into ONBOARDING LITE
       "Not now — stay in Expense"        → closes, nothing switches
  4. The activation CTA lands on /start/business with persona pre-selected
     to "Both" (they keep their buyer half; still changeable). The lite
     flow is idempotent end to end — the seeder skips whatever exists, so
     re-running it as an existing tenant only ADDS the catalog side. After
     the flow's final hard reload, perspective re-derives from the updated
     persona and the tenant lands in Revenue with a real catalog.

Blind switches into an empty Revenue side are no longer possible.
Revenue→Expense stays ungated for now (buyer-side batch, per discussion).
Readiness probe FAILS OPEN: if it errors, the normal switch is allowed —
a wrongly blocked seller is worse than a wrongly allowed empty screen.
