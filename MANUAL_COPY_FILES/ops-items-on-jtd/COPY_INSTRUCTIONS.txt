─── THE MODEL (022a) ───────────────────────────────
 Two jsonb columns on n_jtd, partial GIN (jsonb_path_ops) indexes:
   followups    [] — any number per commitment
   appointments [] — at most ONE open per commitment (slot_already_open)
 Every item carries the same spine: id, kind, status, scheduled_at,
 original_at, assigned_to(_name), set_by {type,id,name,at}, note,
 rescheduled[] (every move, with reason), outcome {code,note,by,at},
 closed_at, created_at, updated_at. Follow-ups add rung/origin;
 appointments add proposed_by (us|customer), proposed_slots, token,
 asked_at, ask_count, customer_response, is_active.
 Follow-up statuses: open | done | cancelled.
 Appointment statuses: proposed | asked | confirmed | customer_proposed |
   no_response | declined | completed | no_show | cancelled
   (legacy view mapping: proposed/asked → requested, confirmed → accepted,
   customer_proposed → rescheduled).
 Every change goes through ONE writer, jtd_item_write: job row FOR UPDATE,
 version bump (optional p_expected_version → version_conflict), one
 n_jtd_history row with details.item_id, actor triple on everything.
 Generic tools on top: jtd_item_add · jtd_item_assign · jtd_item_reschedule
 (keeps original_at, appends rescheduled[]) · jtd_item_close (outcomes:
 follow-up done|cancelled|reached|no_answer|promised|disputed|other;
 appointment completed|cancelled|declined|not_needed|no_show|no_response).
 jtd_ensure_visit_job(event_id) creates the service-visit job for an event
 that never had one (018's insert for one row) so an item always has a
 job to hang on.
