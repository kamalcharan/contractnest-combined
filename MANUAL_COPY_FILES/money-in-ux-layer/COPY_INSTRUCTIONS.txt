THE MODEL (owner decisions, 2026-08-13)
---------------------------------------
  · ONE money workspace per perspective:
      Revenue  → "Money In"  (/money-in)   nav shows only in Revenue
      Expense  → "To Pay"    (/to-pay)     nav shows only in Expense
  · Money In MERGES receivables + invoices. No separate Invoices nav, no
    tabs: one worklist of buyer stories, and each story holds its
    instalments, its invoice documents (chips → paper viewer) and its
    receipts. Search matches names, contracts AND invoice numbers.
  · Design language: product-led briefing, not dashboard furniture. The
    page opens with a spoken headline — "₹58,500 is owed to you.
    ₹40,500 of it is late — 4 buyers, the oldest 43 days." — and the
    underlined numbers ARE the filters. No stat-card row, no table
    headers. Editorial type, mono microlabels, hairlines; brand color
    as emphasis only. Works in light + dark via theme tokens.
  · Receipt-first reality: money that arrived before its paperwork shows
    as a green sentence up top ("₹1,200 has already arrived without
    paperwork — Tejaswinni and one guest") → Generate opens the composer
    pre-filled; the composer's "Already received" card attaches it and
    the invoice is born settled.
  · The invoice document pages keep the EXISTING contract-invoice paper
    design (white printable card, brand accent bars, Description column,
    Payment Summary / Invoice Details sidecar). /invoices/new composes
    on that same paper, editable in place.
  · /invoices (old hub URL) now redirects to /money-in. Composer and
    viewer routes unchanged.
  · VaNi nudges appear as a one-line offer explicitly tagged "coming" —
    no promised automation that doesn't exist yet.
  · To Pay mirrors the language on the expense side; your ContractNest
    subscription appears as a vendor row (V5: plans are contracts),
    linking to the Subscription page.
