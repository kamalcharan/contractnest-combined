─── WHAT IT DOES (rebuilt 2026-09-18 to the PLAYGROUND shell after the owner:
    "you gave me a beautiful UX and what you built is entirely different") ───
 /ops/timeboard (menu: Timeboard; revenue side). The playground made real:
 TOP BAR  ‹ Today › · range · date · Day / Week / Agenda · View as (Everyone ·
          each teammate) · search · [Find a slot] · refresh.
 STRIP    N need you · N clashes · N to place · N awaiting customer · N
          unassigned · carried over → · parked → · VaNi on/off.
 RAIL     "Who": Everyone + each person (avatar, load bar = timed minutes /
          working minutes over the visible days, hours line or "day off",
          clashes) — click = View as. "VaNi" card with the autonomy dial
          Draft / Ask me / Auto (client preference, localStorage).
 BOARD    "To place" tray = every unslotted service in the window + carried,
          GROUPED by contract + block and collapsed by default (fix after the
          first screenshot, 2026-09-18: 27 chips had pushed the grid below the
          fold) — header "27 to place · 22 overdue · 4 contracts", one chip per
          group with ×N and the oldest due, click a group to open its services
          (draggable, one by one), "Show all" opens every group; the tray is
          height-capped and scrolls, so the grid always stays above the fold;
          then the grid — DAY = a column per person + Unassigned + VaNi,
          WEEK = a column per day, AGENDA = the list. Hatched off-hours /
          day off / holiday / leave, now rule, blocks sized by duration,
          side by side on overlap, ⚠ on a clash; a person's follow-ups at
          their due time and the ladder's reminders in VaNi's column (Day).
 SIDE     (400px; the card renders compact so the evidence line does not wrap
          word by word) The selected item = the board's own JobCard with every verb, the
          CONFIRM BAR under it after a drop ("Move to Tue 22 Sep 2 pm · Ravi?"
          → Move / Move & confirm / Keep as is; a confirmed slot reads "This
          slot was confirmed with the customer. Move … and ask them again?"
          → Move & ask / Move, keep confirmed), the clash preview, and Find a
          slot. Otherwise "VaNi suggests" (from the counts: place N, ask M,
          reminders, clashes — buttons on Ask me / Auto, text only on Draft;
          greyed with Open VaNi when VaNi is off) and "Your day" (the signed-
          in user's rows today). A list (carried / parked / a roll-up chip)
          opens here too.
 BACKLOG  "VaNi suggests" leads with the carried-over services: "22 services
          are overdue since 15 Aug with no slot — about 3 working days of work.
          Spread them over the coming days rather than today" with "Find a slot
          for the oldest" (manual, never gated). Spreading them automatically is
          POA batch 4.
 GESTURE  drag a service (tray or grid) onto a time — Day view: onto a person
          (assigns first) → confirm bar → jtd_schedule_visit (proposed, or
          confirmed) → toast with UNDO (puts the previous slot back through
          the same tool; a first placement has no undo — nothing un-slots).
 FIND A SLOT  modal: What (the tray) · Who (any / a technician) · From →
          next free times over 14 days inside working hours, skipping days
          off, leave, holidays and timed services → pick → confirm bar.
 Only services drag. Follow-ups: no item-reschedule API route yet.
