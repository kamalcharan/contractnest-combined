 The page (Money In idiom — situation sentence, lens signals, story rows):
   Sentence  "₹6,000 to confirm — 2 declared payments — ₹1,17,000 overdue
             across 34 buyers. ₹1,66,500 falls due in the next 30 days."
             Every figure is a lens; overdue/upcoming totals come from the
             same get_tenant_receivables summary Money In shows, so the two
             pages cannot disagree.
   Signals   to confirm (amber) · overdue (red) · next 30 days (brand)
   Rows, grouped by WHEN — overdue · today · next 3 days · coming weeks · later:
     • Overdue: ONE row per buyer (all their late instalments, oldest days)
       → Open → the contract page
     • Not yet due, group-session fees: aggregated per due date + cycle
       ("Monthly instalment × 18 members · ₹1,500 each") → Dues →
       /group-sessions.  Other contracts: one row per instalment → Open →
       the contract page
     • Declared payments waiting for the chair (check-in declarations,
       gs_pending_declarations) → Confirm → the existing
       useConfirmDeclaration mutation (gs_confirm_declaration), then
       receivables refetch. Guest fees without an adhoc invoice show
       "needs an invoice before it can be confirmed" and Open instead
       (confirm is a silent no-op for those — see CLAUDE.md guest item).
     • Declared payments against invoices (/api/payments/declarations)
       → Confirm → the existing useConfirmPaymentDeclaration mutation
   Search   name · contract · reference.  "showing: … ✕" chip clears a lens.
   States   Overdue / Due / To confirm.  One in-flight Confirm at a time;
            the button shows a spinner and the others disable meanwhile.
   Not yet  Lane toggle (arrives with the second lane) and Who — Team /
            Mine / Unassigned (arrives with Services; billing has no owner).
   Expense  On the expense perspective the page explains and links To Pay,
            exactly like Money In.
