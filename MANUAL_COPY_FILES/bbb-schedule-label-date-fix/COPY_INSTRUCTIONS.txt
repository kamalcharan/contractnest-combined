WHAT CHANGED (both files):
- The billing-cycle label (e.g. "Monthly 2/9", "Quarterly 3/4") no longer shows the
  trailing "N/M" fraction — display now reads just "Monthly", "Quarterly", etc.
  This was a display-only fix: the stored label text could go stale after a contract
  is restated/pro-rated (e.g. a paid installment still reading "1/12" after the
  contract's total occurrences were corrected to 9), which was confusing members.
  No data was changed — only how the label string is rendered.
- The due-date text next to/under the label is now a visibly larger font:
    RecordPaymentDialog.tsx : date row  9px  -> 11px (+ medium weight)
    EventCard.tsx (full)    : date row  text-xs (12px) -> text-sm (14px) + semibold
- Amounts, dates, sequence numbers, and the separate live "N/M" badge on the Tasks
  timeline card (EventCard full variant) are UNCHANGED — that badge is already
  computed live from sequence_number/total_occurrences, not from the stale text.

WHERE THIS SHOWS UP IN THE APP:
- Record Payment modal (dues checklist) — opened from a contract's invoice / event.
- Contract "Tasks" tab timeline (seller) and "My Services" tab (buyer) — the
  center-line vertical timeline cards.
