WHAT THIS FIXED
─────────────────────────────────
A member paying a due at check-in ON A MEETING DAY hit
  23502 null value in column "session_contract_id"
and, because the function is one transaction, lost their ATTENDANCE too.

Regression from migration 052: it rewrote gs_submit_checkin to add ON CONFLICT
and changed session_contract_id from v_mc to v_tok.contract_id, which is NULL
for every block-scoped token.

Live impact: zero member payment declarations between 27 Jul and 15 Aug 2026.
  25 Jul (pre-052):  35 attendance / 30 declarations
   8 Aug (post-052): 13 attendance /  0 member declarations
