6. CORRECTION — blank means 0, not unlimited
───────────────────────────────────────────────────
FILES: .../BlockWizard/steps/metering/MeteringStep.tsx
       .../utils/catalog-studio/catBlockAdapter.ts

MeteringStep was built on "blank = NULL = unlimited". That is wrong. Owner
ruling: there is no unlimited plan. Every plan states exactly what it may
create and t_tenant_context keeps the balance sheet (granted vs consumed)
against that number — it is the consumption orchestrator.

  - blank now writes 0, not null
  - placeholder "Unlimited" -> "0"
  - meteringLimits type Record<string, number | null> -> Record<string, number>
  - LIMIT_FIELDS cut from 6 fields to 2: Contracts and RFQs

Why only two: the product bills the party that CREATES. The counterparty
consumes the contract or RFQ for free, so consumption is never capped, and
contacts / users / templates are not chargeable events and carry no cap.
Contracts is the seller's meter; RFQs is the buyer's, because only a buyer
raises an RFQ. A seller plan sets Contracts and leaves RFQs at 0; a buyer
plan does the reverse.

This matters: under the old reading, leaving RFQs blank on a seller plan
would have granted every seller UNLIMITED RFQs.

NULL on t_tenant_context.limit_* is now reserved for the exempt platform
tenant (billing_mode='exempt', migration 015) and is never written from
this screen.

9. Step 7 (part 1) — subscribe_tenant_to_plan
───────────────────────────────────────────────────
MIGRATION: 017_subscribe_tenant_to_plan.sql   ** ALREADY APPLIED to prod **

Tenant self-service subscription, one transaction:
  1. creates the subscriber's contact in the PLATFORM tenant's book,
     stamped with source_tenant_id
  2. raises the plan contract under the platform tenant from the template
  3. applies the template's metering blocks to the SUBSCRIBER's
     t_tenant_context (limits, credit_grant_rates, add-on flags)

Why the contact is created here: source_tenant_id is the only link between
"a contact on a contract" and "a tenant account", and NOTHING in the product
writes it (17 of 361 rows have it, all seed data). Creating the contact FROM
the subscribing tenant's own record sets it by construction — which is why
the flow has to be buyer-initiated.

Guards: platform tenant resolved by is_admin flag; plan must be is_active +
is_live matching + is_public + lifecycle 'signed_off'; platform tenant cannot
subscribe to itself; one active plan per tenant (ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED).

  0 events confirms billingOnly suppresses the service event.
