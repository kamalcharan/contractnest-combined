SLICE B — EQUIPMENT FROM REGISTRY + FLYBY NAME FIX
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. A new "What does this cover?" step in the RFQ flow, between Request
     Details and Define Services. It reads the buyer's OWN registry (useAssets
     — same source as the Equipment Registry page), lists equipment and
     facilities with tick + quantity steppers, and writes equipment_details
     (which the mapper already forwards). OPTIONAL: a pure-service request
     ("10 guards, 1 year") picks nothing and continues.

     Why a NEW component (RfqAssetStep), not the wizard's AssetSelectionStep:
     that one answers "which of your CLIENT's assets does this contract cover"
     — it's keyed on a counterparty (useClientAssets({contact_id})) and carries
     the whole "client lists them after signing" flow. An RFQ's equipment is
     the buyer's own, with no counterparty. Bending that component would have
     been riskier than a small focused one. Contracts still use
     AssetSelectionStep unchanged — the wizard picks per record type.

  2. Flyby name-capture fix. The empty-name blocks on your PRJ-1001 happened
     because the step let you continue without naming a custom line. Names are
     editable (the row auto-expands on add with a "Custom line name…" field) —
     nothing was broken in persistence — but nothing REQUIRED them. Now the
     blocks step won't continue in RFQ mode until every line has a name, with
     the hint "Name every line you want quoted."
