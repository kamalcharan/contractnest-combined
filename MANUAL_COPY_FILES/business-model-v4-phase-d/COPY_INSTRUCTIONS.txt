-------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE MIGRATION DOES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. subscribe_tenant_to_plan - three in-place corrections, each anchor
   asserted so a missed anchor raises rather than silently no-ops:
     a. a 'one_time' bucket separate from 'per_creation'
     b. ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED ignores topup_purchase contracts
     c. one-off grants applied immediately via add_credits

2. purchase_topup_template(p_template_id, p_buyer_tenant_id, p_user_id)
   raises a real contract through create_contract_transaction and
   snapshots the pack's grants into metadata.topup_grants, so a later
   re-pricing of the template cannot change what was sold.

3. fn_apply_topup_grants(p_contract_id) + trg_topup_credits_on_payment,
   an AFTER UPDATE OF status trigger on t_invoices. Credits land ON
   PAYMENT, not on purchase.

4. get_tenant_context and trg_fn_plan_contract_lapsed both now exclude
   metadata->>'source' = 'topup_purchase'.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COLLISION THIS HAD TO SOLVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A top-up purchase is a contract raised BY the platform tenant FOR a
subscriber - structurally identical to a plan subscription. Three
existing pieces of logic matched on exactly that shape:

  - subscribe_tenant_to_plan's ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED guard would have
    treated a purchased pack as an existing plan and refused to ever
    sell that tenant a plan again.
  - get_tenant_context resolves the plan as the newest active platform
    contract for the tenant - so a pack bought after subscribing would
    have displayed AS the plan, replacing the real one.
  - trg_plan_contract_lapsed (Phase C) zeroes allowances when such a
    contract expires - so an expiring pack would have wiped the
    tenant's plan limits.

All three are filtered on metadata->>'source' <> 'topup_purchase'. The
filter is an exclusion rather than a requirement that source =
'plan_subscription', so a plan contract created by some other path in
future still counts as a plan.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
A BUG FOUND ON THE WAY (fixed here)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
subscribe_tenant_to_plan folded both metering modes into one bucket:

    ELSIF v_meter->>'mode' IN ('per_creation', 'one_time') ...
        v_grants := v_grants || v_meter->'grants';

...and v_grants is written to credit_grant_rates - the RATE APPLIED ON
EVERY CREATION. A 'one_time' block means "grant N once"; filed there it
becomes "grant N on every contract this tenant ever creates".

No live plan carries such a block, so nothing over-granted. But a credit
pack IS a one_time block, so this had to be right before packs shipped.
