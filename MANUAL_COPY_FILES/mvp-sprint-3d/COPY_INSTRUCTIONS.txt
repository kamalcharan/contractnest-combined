WHAT WAS WRONG — mine, from Sprint 2e
─────────────────────────────────────
  The `perspective` reveal rule unlocked the Revenue/Expense switcher on
  vendorContractCount >= 1. A brand-new buyer tenant has zero non-client
  contracts, so the switcher was hidden — and the switcher is the only way to
  reach the expense side, where a buyer's RFQs and vendor contracts live.

  The rule was circular: the switcher unlocked on having a non-client
  contract, and creating one required the switcher.

  The mistake was assuming every tenant starts on the revenue side and grows
  into the expense side. That describes a seller. For a BUYER the expense side
  is not a later unlock — it is the entire product.

  Persona now decides, and the count is only the fallback for a seller who has
  grown into it:

    buyer / both  -> always shown
    seller        -> shown once a non-client contract exists
    unknown       -> falls back to the count (which fails open)

  Note what is NOT done here: persona is used to GRANT, never to deny. BBB's
  persona is 'seller' and it holds 44 partner contracts, so it keeps the
  switcher through the count — the reason the count stays in the rule at all.
