WHY THE FORWARD DIRECTION WAS ACTIVELY WRONG (this is the bug you hit)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  You reported: "I selected Lifts/Elevators but the equipment list shows the
  whole list of other items." Here is what the data actually says.

  v_resource_templates_by_industry — the view every template list is built
  from — is:

      m_catalog_resource_templates
        JOIN m_catalog_resource_template_industries    (the link table)
      UNION ALL
      templates WHERE scope='universal'  CROSS JOIN every root industry

  So a template only appears under an industry if it has a LINK ROW, or if
  it is universal (in which case it appears under EVERY industry).

  Counted live:

    industry_id       equipment templates   with NO link row
    ----------------  -------------------   ----------------
    lifts_elevators             2                  2
    hvac                        2                  2

  Both of that industry's templates have zero link rows and are not
  universal. They are therefore invisible to the view. Selecting "Lifts &
  Elevators" returns ONLY the 8 universal templates — Air Conditioner,
  Electrical Panel, CCTV, Fire Extinguisher, Laptop, Router, Printer, RO —
  which is exactly the list in your screenshot. Not a cache. Not a filter.
  The data has a hole, and `hvac` has the identical hole.

  Meanwhile the template that carries the lift Knowledge Tree — the one at
  /admin/global-templates/tree/598f5591-e0ed-473b-8e4d-59b25c5b128a, which
  you correctly said has KT — is "Elevator / Lift", and its home industry is
  facility_management, linked to 14 industries, with 8 variants and 23
  checkpoints.

  So the industry a lift company would NAME and the industry that actually
  CARRIES its catalog are different rows. No amount of fixing the industry
  picker changes that. Asking for the equipment removes the guess entirely:
  whatever the tenant picks is, by construction, a template that exists.

HOW THE REVERSE QUERY IS COMPUTED (no new endpoint)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The view emits ONE ROW PER (template, industry). Walking the pages and
  folding rows by template id therefore yields, for free:

      template.industries[]        every industry it is linked to
      template.primaryIndustryId   the row where is_primary = true

  primaryIndustryId IS the reverse answer, and it is what gets saved as the
  served industry.

  Cross-industry templates deliberately do NOT contribute their full link
  list. HVAC System is linked to 16 industries; saving all of them would
  seed catalogs for hospitals, banks and airlines because someone services
  air conditioners. The home industry is the one that owns the Knowledge
  Tree, so it is the one worth serving.

  Fetching before the tenant has any industry:
  /api/resources/resource-templates is scoped server-side by served
  industries — empty at this point. It already accepts an `industry_ids`
  override (the admin global designer uses it), so passing every industry id
  gives the unscoped list with no API/edge/table change. The edge clamps
  `limit` to 100 and slices the view BEFORE de-duplicating, so one page
  returns ~84 distinct templates out of ~199 and silently drops 5 of the 16
  KT-ready ones — measured, not assumed. Pages are therefore walked: page 1
  reports the total, the remaining pages go out in parallel, capped at 8.


WHERE IT HANDS BACK — vani-consent, NOT resource-pick
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Sprint 2 handed off at /onboarding/resource-pick because skipping it left
  the seeder with empty template arrays ("no service blocks found").

  Now express asks that question itself, so resource-pick is genuinely
  redundant rather than skipped. The new screen emits the EXACT route-state
  payload ResourcePickStep emits:

      { selectedEquipmentTemplates, selectedFacilityTemplates,
        selectedServiceTemplates, personaId }

  and makes the same two side-effect calls it makes:

      POST /api/onboarding/selected-resources    (best-effort, seed retries)
      completeVaniStep('resource-pick', {...})

  plus completeVaniStep('industry-selection', {...}) for the screen it also
  stands in for. Nothing downstream can tell the difference:
  vani-consent → vani-intelligence → vani-working → pricing-review →
  terms-conditions → equipment → done all run unmodified.

  Screen count: express now replaces the first EIGHT screens of the long
  chain with TWO.  ~16 screens -> ~9.
