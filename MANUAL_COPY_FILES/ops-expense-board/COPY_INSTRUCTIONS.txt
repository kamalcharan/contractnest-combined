─── WHAT CHANGED ────────────────────────────────────
 contractnest-edge/.../jtd-nucleus/021_ops_board_expense.sql  (LIVE)
 · jtd_ops_board_expense(tenant, is_live, filters, user) — SAME output shape
   as jtd_ops_board (buckets · facets · counts · window · team · ladder ·
   happened · ask_channels) over the contracts this tenant CLAIMED (or is
   being asked to accept). Lanes · kinds (needs-you *):
     payables    bill_overdue* · bill_due · bill_declared (I declared, the
                 seller has not confirmed)
     services    slot_offered* (the seller proposed a time — answer it) ·
                 service_in_progress · service_awaited (planned day passed,
                 the seller has not come) · service_today* · service_scheduled
     acceptance  to_accept* (a contract addressed to me — matched by accessor
                 tenant, accessor email, or the seller-side contact's
                 email / mobile against MY users — waiting for my acceptance)
   Rows carry cnak + seller_name (also placed in buyer_name so the card's
   title reads the same); to_accept rows carry review_link_suffix. Whole
   invoices follow 020's rule (no open job on the contract). Filters:
   window · kinds · lanes · age · slot · q · per-bucket limits.
 · jtd_buyer_respond_slot(tenant, appointment, accept|propose|decline,
   proposed_at, note): the in-app twin of /slot/:token — verifies the
   appointment belongs to a contract this tenant claimed (and the seller is
   never the buyer of its own contract), mints the slot token if missing,
   delegates to visit_slot_respond. Probed live and rolled back: seller
   tenant → not_your_contract · buyer accept with no timed slot → no_slot ·
   buyer propose → proposed_by_you · unknown id → not_your_contract.
   (021b: the first cut tested a flag SELECT INTO had nulled — `IF NOT NULL`
   never fires; replaced by FOUND.)
 contractnest-api
 · GET /api/jtd/collections/board?perspective=expense → the expense reader;
   lanes payables/acceptance accepted. POST /api/jtd/collections/slots/
   :appointmentId/respond {action, proposed_at, note} → the buyer tool.
 contractnest-ui
 · hooks/queries/useCollectionsQueries.ts — kinds/lanes/card fields for the
   expense side; BoardFilters.perspective; useRespondSlot.
 · components/ops/PaySheet.tsx (NEW) — Pay online / declare offline from
   inside the app. Nothing new for money: it resolves the contract's CNAK
   (given, or from the contract) and the claimed grant's secret
   (/api/contracts/my-access/:cnak) and hands over to the review page's
   PublicPaymentSection (Razorpay through the seller's gateway when they
   have one; offline UPI declaration otherwise — the seller confirms).
   Used by the board, the contract page and To Pay.
 · components/ops/JobCard.tsx — the buyer kinds, evidence lines and verbs:
   Pay now / Pay · declare (bills) · Confirm slot / Suggest another time /
   Not needed (slot_offered; Suggest a time on any service with an open
   appointment) · Review & accept (to_accept → /contract-review in-app).
   History link hidden on the expense side (the drawer reads the owner's
   timeline). Revenue cards unchanged.
 · pages/ops/cockpit/Commitments.tsx — perspective-aware: focus strip All ·
   To pay · Services · To accept with "N need you"; headline in the buyer's
   words ("2 need you — 1 bill overdue, 1 slot to answer …"; "1 bill and 2
   services fall due …"); chips per lane; no VaNi chip / Who / channel /
   cycle on expense; no feed; footer points at To Pay and Claim a contract;
   the old "you're on the expense side" guard is gone. Lane focus persists
   per side (ops.board.lane.expense).
 · pages/ops/cockpit/Home.tsx — the board for both sides (the original
   cockpit index.tsx is no longer routed; delete it in a later pass).
 · pages/contracts/detail/index.tsx — buyer view's Financial Health card:
   "Record payment" is gone for the buyer (it wrote the SELLER's ledger);
   "Pay / declare" opens the PaySheet instead.
 · pages/to-pay/index.tsx — "Pay / declare" per vendor (first open bill).
