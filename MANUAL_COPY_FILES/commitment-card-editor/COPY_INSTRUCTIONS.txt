IMPORTANT BEHAVIOR
Taxes are shown from the real catalogue snapshot, not invented sample tax rates.
Apply commits to the wizard state; Save draft/services persists the contract.
Cancel discards local editor changes. Cancelling a just-added FlyBy leaves its
original placeholder: remove it or complete it before saving Services.
No appointments, invoices, messages or contract status transitions are triggered.
The Money chapter and WhatsApp are not implemented in this release.
