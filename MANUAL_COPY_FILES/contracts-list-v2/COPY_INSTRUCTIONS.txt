CONTRACT LIST V2 - CONTACT CLASSIFICATION BADGE FIX
Branch: codex/contracts-list-v1 (local only; incremental package)
Affected submodule: contractnest-ui only. Requires contracts-list-v1.

ROOT CAUSE
The first list used contract_type and otherwise defaulted Revenue rows to Client.
The badge beside a person's name is a CONTACT classification, not necessarily
the contract's commercial type. They are different concepts in the existing code.

FIX
Read the linked tenant-local contact's classifications through the existing API.
Show all recognized saved classifications, including Partner and Team Member.
Do not show a guessed Client badge on missing data, loading, or request failure.
Lookups are scoped to tenant/environment, cancellable and deduplicated per contact.
Shared records never look up the other tenant's private contact IDs.
No contract/contact data, relationship filters, menus, routes, or old screens change.
If saved contract relationships disagree with contact classifications, those remain
separate data; this display fix does not silently reclassify historical contracts.

FILES / EXACT DESTINATIONS
contractnest-ui\src\pages\contracts\experience\ContactClassificationBadge.tsx
  New badge reading the contact's actual saved classification.
contractnest-ui\src\pages\contracts\experience\index.tsx
  Replace the incorrect inline Client/Partner/Vendor fallback with that badge.
