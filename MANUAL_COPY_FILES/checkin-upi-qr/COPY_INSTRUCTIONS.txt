checkin-upi-qr - In-app UPI QR on the check-in page

WHY
---
Deep-link upi:// intents built by our page are unsigned. GPay rejects
personal-VPA payments arriving that way ("Payments to this receiver
are not allowed by UPI network"). The same VPA works when paid via a
scanned QR. This adds a QR render of the same upi:// URL to the page
so users can scan it with any UPI app instead of tapping the intent.
Still keeps the existing "Open UPI app" button + manual reference
fallback so nothing is lost.

Note: even the scanned QR carries the same unsigned URL, so GPay may
still refuse it on personal-account VPAs. The reason we're shipping
this anyway: it costs nothing to try, replaces the "type VPA and amount
manually" flow with "scan and confirm" for the UPI apps that do accept
it (PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM in most cases), and doesn't remove any
existing option.

FILES CHANGED
-------------
  - contractnest-ui/package.json
      Adds qrcode.react ^4.2.0 dependency (~10KB gzipped, MIT, React 19).
  - contractnest-ui/src/pages/checkin/SessionCheckinPage.tsx
      - Import QRCodeSVG from qrcode.react.
      - Inside renderPayBlock, build the full upi://pay?pa=&pn=&am=&cu=&tn=&mc=0000 URL.
      - Render QR above the "Open UPI app" button as primary CTA.
      - Tapping the QR after scanning flips paymentAttempted so the
        reference-input step appears exactly as with the "Open UPI app" tap.
      - "Open UPI app" demoted to outline-style secondary button below an "or" divider.
